## Checks and validation boundaries

Clean bounds-checked serial/MPI slab/pencil and ASan/UBSan builds pass (ASan leak detection disabled). The analytic coupled distributed quadratic reaches its known field/scalar optimum, including non-identity metrics and a four-rank empty owner. Positive-curvature safeguards, fixed scalars, history bounds, invalid/inconsistent options, zero budgets and failed line searches are checked. Real PM tests verify target reduction/fresh evaluation and exact convergence for a null-response prior target. They do not assume arbitrary CIC objectives converge. Linear/LPT, survey Gaussian/Poisson and PM value/full target comparisons pass. Existing posterior/framework regressions pass; options-based optimization also runs on the serial compatibility linear/ZA/2LPT models. See engineering.json; full-library CI was not run.

The initial non-identity-metric test exposed an incorrect H0 scale; it now uses s.y/(y.H_base.y). A sparse N8 nonlinear PM fixture did not converge at 30 or 100 iterations; its integration test now checks improvement/target consistency, and a separate known prior-only PM target tests exact convergence. Its original failed convergence logs remain in local review artifacts. The ill-conditioned analytic quadratic stalled around a few times1e-8 under MPI reductions at a 1e-8 gradient threshold; its unit gradient tolerance is 1e-7 with the original analytic solution-error bound 2e-7. The scientific benchmark tolerance remains2e-5. No claim of convergence is inferred from objective reduction.

Open MPI initialization initially failed because the sandbox blocked local sockets; permission was granted and MPI jobs reran. One existing four-rank PM regression exceeded 120s while sharing the host with the benchmark; the remaining checks reran with a 300s deadline. Original logs and interrupted development attempts are preserved locally. New optimizer options use the model replicated-metadata hook, leaving legacy map_controls semantics intact.

## Limits

One fixed-noise mock and initialization, finite numerical reference, fixed nuisance parameters. Broader seed/amplitude/refinement checks remain deferred. Armijo with skipped unsafe curvature pairs is not Wolfe/global-convergence assurance; CIC assignment faces remain a numerical limitation. No uncertainty/coverage, cosmological, survey-PM or production-scaling claim. Per-algorithm reconstruction diagnostics are generated by the unchanged preceding analyzer. Reports retain every failed criterion and return nonzero if any required comparison criterion fails.
